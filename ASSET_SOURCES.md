Each cover is presented beside its corresponding publication. Desktop cards use two columns and equal-height artwork, retaining each image's original aspect ratio without padded landscape frames. On phones, the full-sized cover sits above its paper title. Clicking it opens an in-page preview with links to the official source and the author's paper. The images identify the conferences; they are not represented as photographs of the owner's attendance or as original artwork. Rights remain with their respective owners.
